## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for a custom furniture modeling and carpentered commerce platform. It balances tactile craftsmanship with high-precision digital tools. The brand personality conveys reliability, warmth, architectural clarity, and approachable professionalism. It addresses both consumers seeking custom pieces for their homes and master carpenters evaluating precise structural specifications.

The visual style combines **Warm Minimalism** with organic Nordic/Japanese craft sensibilities:
- Generous, airy surfaces bathed in warm bone and linen creams.
- Earthy forest pine and deep moss greens evoke raw woodshop precision and natural longevity.
- Natural timber swatches, crisp hairline borders, and pill-shaped segment indicators deliver an intuitive workspace where complex technical 3D configurations feel relaxed, tactile, and immediately comprehensible.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-tech modeling tools in natural, carpentry-inspired materials:

- **Primary (`#2B4C38`)**: Deep forest pine green used for key calls to action, completed flow steps, selected segmented toggles, and primary navigation highlights.
- **Secondary (`#8A6D4B`)**: Mid-tone cedar wood hue, serving secondary actions, interactive wireframe guides, and secondary accent items.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373`)**: Warm honey oak, providing material swatches, 3D transform indicator highlights, and soft focus badges.
- **Neutral (`#1C1C1A`)**: Deep charcoal oak for high-contrast, effortless legibility on cream surfaces, avoiding pure digital black.
- **Surfaces**: Layered transitions moving from canvas `#FAF8F5` to nested card containers `#FFFFFF` and inset measurement tracks `#F5F2EB` / `#EFECE4`.
- **Borders**: Muted stone tints `#EAE6DE` and `#D8D2C6` delineate containers cleanly without high visual weight.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and UI labels. Its geometric foundations combined with rounded, friendly terminal forms mirror contemporary industrial furniture joinery.

- **Headlines**: Weighted heavily (`700` and `800`) with tight letter tracking (`-0.02em`) to command attention above dense parameter controls.
- **Labels & Input Markers**: Set in `600` weight to guarantee optical clarity when reading technical values (e.g., `120 × 90 × 40 cm`, `cm`, `mm`).
- **Body & Supporting Copy**: Kept at balanced line heights (`1.4`–`1.5`) in neutral charcoal or muted slate, ensuring technical notes and disclaimers remain legible at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a studio workstation architecture:

- **Desktop Workspace (≥ 1024px)**: 3-column asymmetric layout with an explicit distribution:
  - Left panel (240px - 280px): Module toolbox, photo upload, and foundational constraints.
  - Central canvas (Flex 1): 3D stage and spatial preview viewport.
  - Right panel (300px - 340px): Dimension inspector, material specifier, and quote/export trigger.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px – 1023px)**: Reflows to a 2-column or collapsable side-drawer setup where the 3D viewport remains dominant.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column vertical stack with persistent bottom step navigation and contextual tab switches between "Paleta", "3D", and "Resumen".
- **Internal Spacing Rhythm**: Built around a base 4px/8px module. Element gaps within toolcards use `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px), while outer card boundaries use `space-lg` (24px) for clear spatial separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile, physical layering rather than dramatic drop shadows:

- **Surface Levels**:
  - `Base`: Canvas tone `#FAF8F5`.
  - `Cards / Workspace Surfaces`: Pure white `#FFFFFF` or stone-tinted containers `#F5F2EB`.
  - `Inset Wells & Input Fields`: Inset containers filled with `#FAF8F5` or `#FFFFFF` paired with structural hairline borders.
- **Shadows**:
  - `Card Flat`: No directional shadow; boundary defined by `1px solid #EAE6DE`.
  - `Interactive Floating Controls`: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 76, 56, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.04)`.
  - `Modal / Active Focus Overlays`: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.12)`.
- **Selected States**: Signaled by a 2px solid ring of `#2B4C38` with an inset `2px` white spacer, avoiding blurred neon halos in favor of crisp woodshop blueprint clarity.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline), complemented by fully rounded pill profiles for directional wayfinding:

- **Main Panels & Cards**: `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) for friendly architectural presence.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Selectors, Module Buttons)**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) providing clear interactive targets.
- **Pill Badges & Step Indicators**: `rounded-full` (`9999px`) for high-level workflow steps, view-mode toggles, and status tags.
- **Material Swatches**: `rounded-lg` (`8px`) square tokens bordered by crisp hairline frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#2B4C38`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, padding `10px 20px`, `rounded-lg`. Hover: `#223E2E`. Active: `#1A3124`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #D8D2C6`, text `#1C1C1A`, hover background `#F5F2EB`.
- **Tool Action Buttons**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE6DE`, `rounded-md`, horizontal alignment with icon + small text.

### Segmented Controls & Tabs
- Container styled as a pill (`rounded-full`) or soft bar (`rounded-lg`) filled with `#F5F2EB` and `p-1`.
- Active segment: Background `#2B4C38` with `#FFFFFF` text or clean white `#FFFFFF` tab with charcoal text and subtle border.

### Stepper Navigation
- Centered horizontal workflow indicator connected by a subtle `1px solid #D8D2C6` hairline.
- Inactive steps: Circular outline badges `#FFFFFF` with charcoal numbering.
- Active step: Solid pill or circle with `#2B4C38` background, white label, and high-contrast typography.

### Input Fields & Dimension Controls
- Inset numeric and text boxes: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8D2C6`, `rounded-md`, padding `8px 12px`.
- Inline units (e.g., `cm`, `mm`): Right-aligned text in muted neutral `#737067`.
- Focus state: Border color `#2B4C38` with a `0 0 0 1px #2B4C38` ring.

### Cards & Tool Palettes
- White background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE6DE`, inner padding `16px` to `20px`, `rounded-xl`.
- Interactive module cards (shelves, panels, drawers): Centered asset render, clean label below, hover lift with border darkening to `#8A6D4B`.

### Material & Color Swatches
- Square aspect ratio (`48px` x `48px`), `rounded-lg`, fine internal border.
- Selected state: Outer ring `2px solid #2B4C38` with `2px` offset margin and an embedded checkmark badge.
